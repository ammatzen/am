6.2 Key Risk Details

Risk 1: AI Platform Algorithm Changes

Description: AI platforms may change how they select, rank, and display content, potentially reducing Zenni's visibility.

Mitigation Actions:

  • Diversify across multiple AI platforms (not over-indexed on one)
  • Maintain strong organic SEO as foundation
  • Build direct relationships with AI platforms
  • Monitor platform changes weekly

Risk 2: Increased Competition

Description: Competitors invest heavily in AI optimization, eroding Zenni's current advantage.

Mitigation Actions:

  • Maintain content quality and freshness advantage
  • Focus on unique value propositions (price, quality, selection)
  • Build strong brand presence in AI platforms
  • Invest in customer experience differentiation

1. Executive Summary

Zenni already owns important product inventory and several root pages (notably Blokz blue-light content and numerous /b/ product-listing pages). That gives us immediate, low-effort SEO upside by converting existing pages into hybrid hubs (concise editorial + shoppable grid + FAQ/schema).

90-day focus: Prioritize three quick wins (blue-light hub upgrade, aviator style guide, kids polarized hub) and deliver 4–6 medium/strategic builds (face-shape landing pages, rimless guide, reading strength chart, face-shape quiz, oversized lookbook).
Opportunity: Executing the prioritized plan aims to capture a large portion of the estimated 100,000+ monthly non-branded searches currently dominated by Warby Parker and Zeelool.

3. Unified Findings & Patterns

Market structure

Incumbents (Warby Parker, LensCrafters) capture heavy volume with hybrid category hubs + robust schema; challengers (Zeelool) grow quickly through targeted category pages and fit/style tools. Zenni's advantage is inventory and /b/ product listings — the gap is editorial and utility content that connects search intent to product pages.

Top tactical weaknesses

  • Lack of hybrid landing pages for several high-volume queries (blue-light hubs are partial; face-shape pages partial or missing).
  • Competitors use FAQ schema, product/offer schema, and interactive tools (try-on, quizzes), which boost CTR and conversion.

Conversion opportunity

Upgrade existing pages first (low dev cost), then build new assets (higher dev cost). Shoppable editorial hubs (short editorial + product grid + FAQ schema) consistently outperform plain product listings for competitive keywords.

8. Implementation Checklist & Handoff Notes

Developer & SEO (per new/updated page)

  • JSON-LD for Product / Offer / FAQPage / HowTo as appropriate
  • Shoppable grid links correctly to /b/ SKUs (UTM per tile)
  • Page-level canonical and sitemap updated
  • Mobile-responsive hero and product grid; LCP images optimized
  • AR try-on integrated on product tiles (if available)
  • Internal links from main nav, category pages, and related blog posts
  • New/updated pages submitted to GSC; monitor for errors

Content & creative

  • Final copy (approved H1/H2/H3) and imagery per brief
  • PDF asset designed and accurate at 100% print scale
  • Short PD video (1–2 min) with captions and thumbnail
  • QA for accessibility and SEO (headings, alt text, ARIA where needed)

Project handoff

Provide each brief in the project tracker: title, owner, est. days, acceptance criteria, live URL placeholder. Include: canonical, meta title, meta description, target keywords, and required JSON-LD snippet.
